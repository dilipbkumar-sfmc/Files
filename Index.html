What is Agentforce Marketing?

Agentforce Marketing is Salesforce's marketing solution for the Agentic Enterprise. AI agents help marketers plan, create campaigns and content, optimize, and orchestrate adaptive customer experiences across every channel and interaction. Built on the world's #1 CDP, Agentforce Marketing connects data, AI, automation, and engagement into one deeply unified platform for B2B and B2C marketing.
